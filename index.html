Welcome to Aura Intelligence.

Questions, Answered

Frequently asked questions.

Do I need prior coding experience to join?

No. The program removes all technical jargon and is built for absolute beginners. All you need is the willingness to learn — the curriculum starts from zero.

How long is the program and how often are classes held?

The program runs for 6 months across 80 classes, held on alternate days for a maximum of 3 classes per week, each 1.5 hours long.

Is the course available online, or only offline in Tripura?

Both. The program runs offline in Agartala, Tripura, and online live across India via Google Meet — same curriculum, same outcomes.

What do I actually get at the end of the program?

A verified certification proving mastery in generative AI tools, strategy, and architecture — earned by completing four capstone projects across the 6 months.

What is the A.U.R.A framework?

A.U.R.A stands for Awakening, Unreal, Real, and Architect — the four pillars the curriculum is built on, moving from AI fundamentals to creative generation, applied coding, and finally monetization.

What's the difference between the CBCE and General Student fee?

CBCE Skill Development is a special subsidized partner rate (₹11,700 total). General Students pay the standard rate (₹14,500 total). Both include the same full curriculum and certification.
